Vishwas Billing

A private Windows app for New Vishwas Enterprises to collect billing evidence and reconcile the shop’s expenses.

What it does

The app produces a local report for the shop owner. It compares provider usage with available invoices and payment evidence, tracks estimates separately, and shows where evidence is incomplete.

Why it uses Gmail

With authorization, the app uses the Gmail API to check a dedicated Vishwas Billing label for supported billing PDFs and Meta Ads receipts. A separate connection can collect Axis Bank Visa 0198 billing evidence. Google grants read-only permission for each mailbox. The app uses each mailbox’s label to limit its searches.
