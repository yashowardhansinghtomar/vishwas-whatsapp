Vishwas Billing privacy policy

New Vishwas Enterprises operates Vishwas Billing as a private desktop app for the shop’s billing records.

Permission and data accessed

The shop owner and the authorized cardholder can separately authorize Gmail read-only access through Google. Google grants this permission at the mailbox level. The app checks the email address to confirm the enrolled mailbox and verifies its dedicated Vishwas Billing label. It reads label names and IDs during setup to locate that label. The shop mailbox search is for billing PDFs and Meta business receipts.

For matching messages, the app fetches the message details, which can include Gmail message IDs, labels, timestamps, headers, body content and attachment details. It reads the body of supported Meta Ads receipts and downloads PDFs from matching messages for validation. Billing data can include account, invoice and transaction references, amounts, taxes and payment references. All mailbox operations use read-only Gmail API methods.

With separate permission, the Visa 0198 connection searches the enrolled cardholder mailbox’s billing label for Axis Bank charge alerts, WhatsApp Business AutoPay reminders, and encrypted Visa 0198 monthly statements. It uses selected billing fields from matching alerts and reminders, including the card ending, merchant, amount, dates and evidence identifiers. Alert text, credit limits and unrelated alert details are excluded from the local billing evidence.

Use and storage

The app uses Gmail data to identify and validate billing evidence, avoid counting the same document twice, compare bills and payments, and prepare a local report for the shop owner. The Windows app saves the refresh credential and label ID locally so it can run after the initial authorization. The local billing ledger retains accepted PDF files, decoded text and identifiers from accepted receipts, extracted billing fields, dates, and evidence hashes. Unsupported evidence is rejected. The app can retain its hash and rejection reason.

The ledger and generated HTML, JSON and CSV reports are kept on the authorized Windows computer under the Windows user account that runs the app. Accepted Gmail PDFs and receipt text are stored inside the ledger database. Copies remain there until they are removed. Removing a Gmail label or revoking access leaves existing local copies in place.

When statement unlocking is enabled, the local ledger keeps each accepted monthly statement PDF in its original encrypted form. The complete encrypted PDF may contain personal transactions unrelated to Vishwas. The app opens it locally and extracts only supported Meta transaction rows into the ledger and reports. A short unlock prefix and a separate Gmail credential are stored in the local Windows credential directory for recurring reads.

Sharing and limited use

The Windows app communicates with Google to authorize access and retrieve the selected billing evidence. The retrieved evidence stays on the authorized Windows computer. The shop owner can review the local records. An operator may inspect a specific record for billing support with the owner’s explicit permission. The app does not send Gmail data to AI services or use it for AI training.

Vishwas Billing uses information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnect Gmail or remove local copies

Each mailbox owner can independently revoke Vishwas Billing’s access from that mailbox’s Google Account connections. This stops future access for that mailbox. To remove the saved connection from the authorized Windows computer, delete its local credential from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vishwas\Billing\credentials. The shop credential is gmail_authorized_user.json and the card credential is gmail_card_authorized_user.json. The cardholder can also remove axis_statement_prefix.json to disable scheduled statement unlocking.

Contact shop support for help accessing the local reports or removing stored copies. To clear the app’s local billing history, disconnect Gmail, stop the Windows billing task, and remove billing.sqlite3, its -wal, -shm or -journal companion files if present, and the contents of the reports folder from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vishwas\Billing or the configured billing folder. This clears billing history from other sources kept in those files as well.

Files separately placed in the billing inbox, archived outside the app, or exported elsewhere must be handled separately. Review applicable accounting retention requirements before removing records. Gmail originals stay in Gmail.
