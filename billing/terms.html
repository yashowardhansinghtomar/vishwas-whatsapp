Vishwas Billing terms of use

New Vishwas Enterprises provides Vishwas Billing for the shop owner’s internal billing review.

Use of the app

Only connect a shop account you are authorized to access. Keep the connection credentials and local billing reports private. The public website provides information about the Windows app. Setup and billing review take place on the authorized Windows computer.

Billing evidence and estimates

The app collects available evidence and shows estimates and reconciliation differences. Coverage depends on the records each source makes available. Check original invoices and statements when an official amount or payment status is needed. Payments and billing settings are managed through the relevant provider.

Gmail authorization

Connecting Gmail is optional. See the privacy policy for the data used and how to disconnect or remove local copies.
